What is CampingMath?

Real math for real campsites

CampingMath is part of a small family of practical planning sites, alongside Backyard Math and Split Math. Instead of guessing how much firewood to buy or whether your tent will actually fit on the pad, you get a straightforward estimate built from transparent formulas — plus the assumptions behind every number, so you know exactly how much to trust it.

What size tent do I actually need?

Manufacturer "person ratings" describe how many sleeping bags can be squeezed onto the floor shoulder-to-shoulder — not how comfortable it'll feel with gear, pets, and pads inside. Our Tent Size Calculator estimates real sleeping footprints so you can size up with confidence.
